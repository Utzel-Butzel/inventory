# Generated demo pictures

The five item `generated-*.webp` assets were created on 2026-10-31 with the built-in
Imagegen tool, one generation per object, and encoded as WebP without changing
the composition. They depict fictional inventory, not real products or stock.
The seed uses only these generated images. Checksums and dimensions are in
`scripts/demo-seed-manifest.mjs`. No image generation occurs in the running demo.

The item prompts use `product-mockup`, an inventory demo item photograph, landscape
3:2 framing, natural detailed materials and soft daylight. Full prompts:

## generated-drill.webp

Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: inventory demo item photograph
Primary request: photorealistic catalog photo of a cordless 18V drill with two batteries in an open fitted black tool case on a tidy wooden workshop bench.
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, complete tool case visible, three-quarter view, natural detailed materials, soft daylight.
Constraints: fictional unbranded tool, no people, no text, no logos, no watermark. This image illustrates a fictional inventory demo.

## Location pictures (2026-11-01)

Three additional images were created with the built-in Imagegen tool, one per
location, and encoded as 1536 × 1024 WebP at quality 85 without cropping or
changing the composition. Every demo resource now has its own cover image.
The seed validates complete coverage; the deployment smoke check verifies
media associations, cover positions, alt text, content types, and file checksums.

### generated-shelf.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: inventory demo location photograph, Regal A2
Primary request: photorealistic overview photograph of a sturdy open galvanized steel workshop shelving unit with warm wooden shelves against a pale plaster wall in a tidy small makerspace. Black fitted tool cases and cordless drill accessories occupy the lower shelves; neat blue open-front bins of screws, nuts and washers and a grey tray of black cable ties occupy the middle shelf. Show the complete shelving unit as the main subject, with enough workshop context to recognize it as a storage location rather than a product shot.
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, eye-level three-quarter view, straight verticals, complete shelves clearly visible, natural detailed steel, wood and plastic textures, soft window daylight. Cohesive blue, grey and warm wood palette.
Constraints: fictional unbranded workshop, no people, no readable text, no labels, no logos, no watermark. One single photograph, no collage. This illustrates a fictional inventory demo.

### generated-workbench.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: inventory demo location photograph, Werkbank E1
Primary request: photorealistic overview photograph of a complete electronics workstation in a tidy small makerspace: a wide light wooden workbench with a large blue antistatic mat, compact charcoal soldering station with its iron safely in the stand, brass tip cleaner, solder reel, handheld dark digital multimeter and neatly coiled red and black leads. A small fume extractor, adjustable task lamp, component drawers and neatly hung hand tools make it a practical repair and prototyping station. Include a simple stool tucked beneath the workbench. Show the workstation and its surroundings as a place, with clear open working space.
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, eye-level three-quarter room view, whole workbench visible, realistic proportions and fine natural material textures, soft window daylight against a pale wall. Cohesive blue, grey and warm wood palette.
Constraints: fictional unbranded workshop, no people, no readable text, no logos, no watermark, no sparks or smoke. One single photograph, no collage. This illustrates a fictional inventory demo.

### generated-parts-bin.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: inventory demo location photograph, Fach K3
Primary request: photorealistic medium-wide photograph of one organized small-parts storage compartment within a galvanized steel workshop shelving unit with warm wooden shelves. The full shelf compartment is framed by its metal uprights and the shelf above. Several blue open-front parts bins hold stainless steel socket head screws, nuts and washers, with a shallow grey tray of black cable ties alongside. The front edges of the bins are aligned and easy to reach. Show the entire compartment as the storage location, with visible shelf boundaries and a little of the surrounding shelving, rather than a macro photograph of individual screws.
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, nearly frontal view with slight depth, all containers in focus, crisp natural steel, plastic and wood textures, soft window daylight against a pale wall. Cohesive blue, grey and warm wood palette.
Constraints: fictional unbranded workshop, no people, no readable text, no labels, no logos, no watermark. One single photograph, no collage. This illustrates a fictional inventory demo.
